# Sharpening Chisels

A sharp chisel is the foundation of fine woodworking. Start with a coarse
grit to establish the primary bevel, then progress to finer stones.

## Honing Guide

1. Set the primary bevel to 25 degrees
2. Hone on a 1000-grit waterstone
3. Strop with green compound on leather

A dull blade requires more force and tears the fibers instead of slicing
them cleanly.
