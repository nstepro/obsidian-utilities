# Garden Layout

The raised beds run north to south so tomatoes do not shade the peppers.
Beans climb the trellis on the eastern fence where morning sun hits first.

Crop rotation plan: legumes follow the leafy greens because they fix
nitrogen; root vegetables take the freed bed the year after.

Compost: alternate green material (grass clippings) with brown (dry
leaves), keep it moist like a wrung sponge, and turn it weekly.
