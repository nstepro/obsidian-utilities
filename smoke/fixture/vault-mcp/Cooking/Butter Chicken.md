# Butter Chicken Recipe

Butter chicken is a classic North Indian dish. Marinate chicken thighs in
yogurt, ginger, and garlic, then simmer in a creamy tomato sauce.

## Marinade

- 500 g chicken thighs
- 150 g yogurt
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1 tsp kashmiri chili powder

## Sauce

- 400 g tomatoes, pureed
- 50 g butter
- 100 ml cream
- 1 tsp garam masala

Simmer the sauce for twenty minutes before adding the grilled chicken.
