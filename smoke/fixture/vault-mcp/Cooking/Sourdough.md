# Sourdough Starter

Day one: mix 100 g flour with 100 ml water in a jar. Leave uncovered.

Day three: bubbles should appear — feed with equal weights flour and water
every twenty-four hours. Discard half before each feeding.

By day seven the starter should double within six hours and smell sourly
sweet. Now it is ready to leaven bread.

# Baking the Loaf

Stretch and fold the dough every half hour for three hours, then cold
retard overnight. Bake in a preheated Dutch oven at 250 °C with the lid on
for twenty minutes, then uncovered until deeply browned.
